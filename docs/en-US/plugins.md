# Plugins and Extensions

- **Swashbuckle.AspNetCore** — Swagger/OpenAPI generation at `/swagger`.
- **Serilog.AspNetCore** — structured console logging.
- **OpenTelemetry** — traces, metrics, and logs instrumentation.
- **Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience** — retry, circuit breaker, and timeout for `HttpClient`.
- **AutoMapper** — mapping between external entities and DTOs.
- **FluentValidation** — model validation (available in Proxy, not yet used).
